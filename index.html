Dear Kal, 💗

Happy birthday to one of my favorite humans. I hope this year is gentle with you, surprises you in the best ways, and gives you a hundred reasons to smile when you least expect it.

Thank you for being part of my life. For the laughs, the random conversations, the memories, and even the moments when we do absolutely nothing and somehow it's still fun. I'm glad I get to know you, cheer for you, and see you become more and more yourself.

Happy birthday, Kal.
